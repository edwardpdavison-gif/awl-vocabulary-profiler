How to use this tool

Paste a reading text into the box and click Analyse text. The tool compares the vocabulary in the text with two word lists.

  • GSL: General Service List (West, 1953), a list of common high-frequency English words.
  • AWL: Academic Word List (Coxhead, 2000), a list of word families commonly found in academic texts.
  • Off-list: words not currently matched to either list.

A higher percentage of AWL or off-list vocabulary may suggest that a text is more challenging, but the result should be used as a guide rather than a final judgement.

The app will show the user which AWL words appear in their text, organised by sublist. 'Off-list' words will also be shown, these words may present challenges for thier EAL students

Please note

  • The GSL was complied in 1953. Many very common modern words will therefore appear as off-list, as will proper nouns.
  • This tool is intended as a teacher support tool, not a complete readability measure.
